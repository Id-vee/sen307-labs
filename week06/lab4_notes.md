# Lab 4 Notes – Referential Integrity

During Part 4 of the practical, I encountered **Error 1451**, which occurred when I attempted to delete the Library and Information Science department while a student was still assigned to it. MySQL refused the deletion because of the foreign key constraint between the Department and Student tables. This constraint ensures referential integrity by preventing a parent record from being deleted while a child record still references it. I fixed the problem by first updating the student's department to Software Engineering and then deleting the Library and Information Science department successfully. Another possible solution would have been to delete the dependent student record first, although this would mean losing the student's record. Alternatively, if the database schema were designed with an appropriate `ON DELETE CASCADE` rule, deleting the department could automatically delete its dependent student records.
